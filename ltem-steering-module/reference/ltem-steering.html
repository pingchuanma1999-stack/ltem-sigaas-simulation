泵浦光斑往哪走Where the pump spot goes

泵浦光被盒外的电动转向镜反射（镜面竖直，平时只用水平转动），从右侧壁入射口水平进入 AFM。盒内一块竖直平面镜（在针尖后方）把它水平折向前方，打到针尖前方抛物面镜的右半边，再聚焦到下方的样品。针尖散射的信号由抛物面镜左半边收集，经另一块竖直平面镜从左侧出去。下文的「前」指盒子开口、人站的那一侧（照片 1 拍摄的位置），「右」指入射口那一侧。

The pump beam reflects off the motorized steering mirror outside the box (mirror face vertical; normally only the horizontal rotation is used) and enters the AFM horizontally through the port in the right wall. Inside, a vertical flat mirror behind the tip folds it horizontally toward the front, onto the right half of the parabolic mirror in front of the tip, which focuses it down onto the sample. The tip-scattered signal is collected by the left half of the parabolic mirror and leaves through the left wall via another vertical flat mirror. Below, “front” is the open side of the box where you stand (where photo 1 was taken) and “right” is the entrance-port side.

两根电机Two actuators

按 Thorlabs KS1-Z8 图纸，从电机一侧看，手动旋钮（枢轴）在左下。和它同高的下面那根只产生水平转动，它正上方的上面那根只产生竖直转动。两根绕不同的轴转，所以光斑走的方向不同。数值是推杆沿轴线的伸出量，也就是 KDC101 读数的变化（0.001 mm = 1 µm）。

Per the Thorlabs KS1-Z8 drawing, seen from the actuator side the manual knob (the pivot) is at the lower left. The lower actuator, level with it, only tilts the mirror horizontally; the upper one, directly above it, only tilts it vertically. They rotate the mirror about different axes, so they move the spot in different directions. Values are the push-rod extension along its axis, i.e. the change in the KDC101 reading (0.001 mm = 1 µm).

光斑形状Spot shape

入射按圆形高斯光束计算。光束直径默认取抛物面镜口径下光斑最小的值，可在下方参数里改。光线逐条经过转向镜、盒内平面镜和抛物面镜，再把抛物面镜上各点出射的平面波在样品面上叠加，即标量衍射。强度以原位光斑的中心强度为 100%。

The input is a circular Gaussian beam. By default its diameter is the one that gives the smallest spot for the parabolic-mirror aperture; you can change it in the parameters below. Rays are traced one by one through the steering mirror, the fold mirror and the parabolic mirror, and the plane waves leaving each point of the parabolic mirror are summed on the sample plane (scalar diffraction). Intensities are relative to the original spot's peak = 100%.

在实验台上确认，大约 5 分钟Check on the bench, about 5 minutes

  1. 在入射口外放一张红外显示卡，水平电机点动 +200 µm，看卡上光点往前还是往后移（按图纸应往前，250 mm 外约 2.7 mm；看完按相反方向走回原位）。往前 → 读数增大时光斑往左；往后 → 往右。这一步也能确认被圈的控制器接的是不是下面那根。
  2. 把步长改成 50 µm，在 AFM 顶视相机里走一步再走回来。按默认参数光斑约移动 45 µm（左右 43、前后 16），一定看得见。量出真实位移，就有了你们自己的 µm/µm 标定，可以替换下面的 R、L。
  3. 参数里默认按"光斑最小"自动取光束直径，这个值由抛物面镜口径决定。如果量到的实际直径不同，取消"自动"后手动填。光斑变形快慢对它最敏感。
  4. 相机里原位光斑是沿入射面拉长的椭圆（俯视是对角线）。水平电机走到 ±10 µm 时，按默认参数椭圆会明显变圆变大，可以顺便核对直径设得对不对。
  5. 做非局域扫描时，每个点都从同一个方向走到位。Z812 标称回差 < 8 µm，和整段非局域扫描的电机行程（几微米）同一量级，换向后的前几微米镜子可能根本没动。
  1. Hold an IR viewer card just outside the port and jog the horizontal actuator by +200 µm. Watch whether the dot moves toward the front or the back (per the drawing it should move toward the front, about 2.7 mm at 250 mm; then jog back the same way). Front → the spot moves left as the reading increases; back → right. This also tells you whether the circled controller drives the lower actuator.
  2. Set the step to 50 µm and step once and back while watching the AFM top-view camera. With the default parameters the spot moves about 45 µm (43 left–right, 16 front–back), which you will certainly see. Measuring the real displacement gives your own µm/µm calibration, which can replace R and L below.
  3. By default the beam diameter is set automatically for the smallest spot, which depends on the parabolic-mirror aperture. If your measured diameter differs, untick “Auto” and enter it. How fast the spot deforms is most sensitive to this value.
  4. In the camera the original spot is an ellipse stretched along the plane of incidence (a diagonal in top view). At ±10 µm on the horizontal actuator, with the default parameters, it becomes clearly rounder and larger, which is a quick check of the diameter setting.
  5. During a non-local scan, approach every point from the same direction. The Z812's rated backlash (< 8 µm) is on the order of the actuator travel of a whole non-local scan (a few µm), so after a reversal the mirror may not move at all for the first few µm.

两根电机为什么方向不同Why the two actuators move the spot differently

  • 镜架：KS1-Z8 从背面看，手动旋钮（枢轴）在左下。下面那根在它右边、同一高度，伸出时镜框绕过旋钮的竖直轴转，从上往下看是逆时针。上面那根在它正上方，伸出时绕过旋钮的水平轴转，镜面往下倾。Z8 手册里 forward 限位在完全伸出处，读数增大就是伸出。
  • 镜面竖直，水平转 δ，出射光在水平面内转 2δ，和入射角 α 无关。推杆沿自身轴线伸出 Δ（就是 KDC101 读数的变化），顶在离枢轴 L 处的镜框上，所以 δ = Δ/L。读数是由电机编码器计数换算的（1 mm = 34,555 个计数，约 29 nm/计数），不是在推杆顶端直接测的，所以换向时会有回差。
  • 盒内的竖直平面镜把光水平折向前方，同时把左右翻过来：入射口处往前偏，射向抛物面镜的光就往左偏。
  • 抛物面镜在针尖前方，轴沿前后方向、平行于样品面，开口朝向针尖。射入光往左偏 Δφ，样品上光斑也往左移 R·Δφ，正好与 β 无关；斜入射再带出一个往前的分量。
  • Mount: seen from the back of the KS1-Z8, the manual knob (pivot) is at the lower left. The lower actuator sits to its right at the same height; extending it rotates the mirror about a vertical axis through the knob, counter-clockwise seen from above. The upper actuator sits directly above the knob; extending it rotates the mirror about a horizontal axis through the knob, tilting the face down. The Z8 manual puts the forward limit at full extension, so a rising reading means extension.
  • The mirror face is vertical, so a horizontal tilt δ turns the reflected beam by 2δ in the horizontal plane, independent of α. The push rod extends by Δ along its own axis (the change in the KDC101 reading) and pushes the mirror at distance L from the pivot, so δ = Δ/L. The reading is computed from the motor encoder (1 mm = 34,555 counts, about 29 nm per count), not measured at the rod tip, hence the backlash on reversal.
  • The vertical fold mirror in the box folds the beam horizontally toward the front and swaps left and right: a deviation toward the front at the port becomes a deviation to the left toward the parabolic mirror.
  • The parabolic mirror sits in front of the tip, its axis along front–back and parallel to the sample, opening toward the tip. A beam deviated left by Δφ moves the spot left by R·Δφ, independent of β; oblique incidence adds a component toward the front.
Δx = 2R · Δ/L
Δy = 2R · Δ/L · sinθ·sinβ / (1 + sinθ·cosβ)
  • 竖直转 δ，出射光往下偏 2δ·cosα。到了抛物面镜，这个偏转落在入射面内，光斑沿入射面朝抛物面镜一侧走。沿入射面的位移还被斜入射拉长 1/cosθ，所以每 µm 走得更远（约 1.18 µm，水平是 0.91 µm）。两个方向在样品面上相差约 108°，不是 90°。
  • 水平转动的位移大部分垂直于入射面，离焦小。竖直转动几乎全沿入射面，离焦大。这也是两者光斑变形快慢不同的原因。
  • A vertical tilt δ deflects the beam downward by 2δ·cosα. At the parabolic mirror this deviation lies in the plane of incidence, so the spot moves along that plane toward the mirror. Oblique incidence also stretches the along-plane displacement by 1/cosθ, so each µm moves the spot farther (about 1.18 µm, versus 0.91 µm for the horizontal actuator). The two directions differ by about 108° on the sample, not 90°.
  • Horizontal tilts move the spot mostly across the plane of incidence, so there is little defocus. Vertical tilts move it almost entirely along the plane, so the defocus is large. That is also why the spot deforms at very different rates.

光斑为什么会变形Why the spot deforms

  • 原位就是椭圆：60° 斜入射时，样品面上的光斑沿入射面被拉长 1/cosθ = 2 倍。
  • 离焦：抛物面镜把光聚在一个垂直于光束的面上，样品面和这个面斜着相交在针尖处。光斑沿入射面移开 s∥，样品面就离焦约 s∥·sinθ。竖直电机走 5 µm，光斑沿入射面移约 6 µm、离焦约 5 µm，已超过瑞利长度（当前参数约 2 µm），所以变大变暗得快。
  • 离轴像差：光束一偏离抛物面镜的轴，离轴抛物面镜就产生像散和彗差，随偏角线性增大。水平电机的离焦小，这一项和离焦差不多大。光斑先变圆，走得更远后长轴转到接近垂直入射面的方向。
  • 光束直径的影响最大：直径越大，原位光斑越小，但瑞利长度按 1/D² 变短、像差随 D 增长，变形更快。光路按最小光斑优化后，光束基本填满抛物面镜的口径，原位光斑最小，但对离焦也最敏感。所以这里的变形比细光束时明显。
  • 对实验的意义：非局域扫描尽量用水平电机。光斑移开几微米以后，泵浦中心强度（激发密度）会下降，定量分析时要考虑，可以用上面的曲线做修正。
  • It starts as an ellipse: at 60° incidence the spot on the sample is stretched along the plane of incidence by 1/cosθ = 2.
  • Defocus: the parabolic mirror focuses onto a plane perpendicular to the beam, which meets the tilted sample plane only at the tip. Moving the spot by s∥ along the plane of incidence puts the sample about s∥·sinθ out of focus. With the vertical actuator at 5 µm the spot moves about 6 µm along the plane and the defocus is about 5 µm, beyond the Rayleigh length (about 2 µm with the current parameters), so the spot grows and dims quickly.
  • Off-axis aberrations: as soon as the beam leaves the mirror axis, the off-axis parabola adds astigmatism and coma, growing linearly with the angle. For the horizontal actuator the defocus is small and this term is comparable to it. The spot first becomes rounder; farther out its long axis turns toward the direction across the plane of incidence.
  • Beam diameter matters most: a larger diameter gives a smaller original spot, but the Rayleigh length shrinks as 1/D² and the aberrations grow with D, so it deforms faster. With the optics optimized for the smallest spot, the beam nearly fills the parabolic-mirror aperture: the original spot is as small as it gets, and also as sensitive to defocus as it gets. That is why the deformation here is stronger than for a thin beam.
  • For the experiment: use the horizontal actuator for non-local scans. Once the spot has moved a few µm, the pump peak intensity (excitation density) drops; account for this in quantitative analysis, for example by correcting with the curve above.

模型假设Model assumptions

  • 盒内光路：两块竖直平面镜在针尖后方（背板一侧），把水平光束水平折向前方；抛物面镜和样品台在它们前方，抛物面镜在针尖前方（开口一侧），右半边照明、左半边收集，收集到的信号经另一块平面镜从左侧壁出去。抛物面镜按 neaspec 专利的半抛物面处理：轴平行于样品面，焦点在镜面外侧。
  • 转向镜镜面竖直，入射光和出射光都平行于桌面，入射光从桌子后方来。入射口那片元件按平板窗口处理；如果它是透镜，以相机实测为准。
  • 两篇论文都给出 1.1″ 对应约 55 nm（2021 年那篇用简单光线光学，2023 年那篇用 AFM 内部光学的光线矩阵，并写明调的是 pitch）。同样 1.1″ 在本模型里约 0.18 µm（左右 0.17、前后 0.06，R = 16 mm）。55 nm ÷ 5.3 µrad ≈ 10 mm，相当于直接拿镜面转角乘约 10 mm 的焦距；光束偏转其实是镜面转角的 2 倍，即使 R 取 10 mm，本模型也给出约 0.11 µm。定量标定请以相机实测为准，方向结论不受这些参数影响。
  • 光斑形状：标量衍射，不含偏振；入射是理想的圆形 TEM00 高斯光束；各镜面没有面形误差。假设两根电机都在 0 时光束正好平行于抛物面镜的轴、焦点落在针尖下的样品面上。如果原位本身就带偏角，变形从一开始就存在。
  • 抛物面镜口径：按以对准时主光线为中心、半角 arcsin(NA) 的圆锥处理，镜面下沿离样品 1 mm；圆锥以外的光被挡掉。光束随转向镜移动时，口径固定不动。"光斑最小"定义为单位激光功率下样品上的峰值光强最大，这是高斯光束填充圆形口径的经典最优：1/e² 半径约为口径半径的 0.89 倍。如果按半高宽最小来调，会把光束放得更大（过填充），半高宽只再小几个百分点，却损失功率。
  • 3D 视图只为看方向：镜面转角和样品上的位移按所选倍数放大，入射段光路按真实角度画，光斑椭圆按算出的半高宽相对缩放。读数、两个示波器视图和光斑形状都是真实尺度的计算。
  • Inside the box: two vertical flat mirrors behind the tip (back-panel side) fold the horizontal beam horizontally toward the front; the parabolic mirror and the sample stage are in front of them, with the parabolic mirror in front of the tip (open side). Its right half illuminates and its left half collects; the collected signal leaves through the left wall via the other flat mirror. The parabolic mirror is modelled as the semi-paraboloid of neaspec's patent: axis parallel to the sample, focus outside the mirror.
  • The steering mirror's face is vertical; the incoming and outgoing beams are parallel to the table, and the incoming beam arrives from the back of the table. The element in the entrance port is treated as a flat window; if it is a lens, trust the camera measurement.
  • Both papers quote about 55 nm for 1.1″ (the 2021 paper from simple ray optics, the 2023 paper from a ray-matrix analysis of the AFM optics, explicitly for pitch). The same 1.1″ gives about 0.18 µm in this model (0.17 left–right, 0.06 front–back, R = 16 mm). 55 nm ÷ 5.3 µrad ≈ 10 mm, i.e. the mirror angle times a ~10 mm focal length; but the beam turns by twice the mirror angle, so even with R = 10 mm this model gives about 0.11 µm. Calibrate with the camera; the directions do not depend on these parameters.
  • Spot shape: scalar diffraction without polarization; the input is an ideal circular TEM00 Gaussian beam; the mirrors have no figure errors. With both actuators at 0 the beam is assumed to be exactly parallel to the parabolic-mirror axis and focused on the sample under the tip. If the original position already carries a tilt, the deformation is there from the start.
  • Parabolic-mirror aperture: a cone of half-angle arcsin(NA) around the aligned chief ray, with the mirror's lower edge 1 mm above the sample; light outside the cone is lost. The aperture stays fixed while the steering mirror moves the beam. “Smallest spot” means the highest peak intensity on the sample per unit laser power, the classic optimum for a Gaussian beam filling a circular aperture: 1/e² radius ≈ 0.89 × aperture radius. Tuning for the smallest FWHM instead would enlarge the beam further (overfill): the FWHM shrinks only a few percent more while power is lost.
  • The 3D view is for directions only: mirror angles and spot displacements are exaggerated by the selected gain, the input path is drawn at true angles, and the spot ellipse is scaled by the computed FWHM ratio. The readouts, both scopes and the spot-shape panel are true-scale calculations.
